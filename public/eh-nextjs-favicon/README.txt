EH Electric HVAC - Next.js favicon assets

For Next.js App Router:
1. Put favicon.ico, icon.png, and apple-icon.png in your app/ folder.
   Next.js will automatically use these as metadata icons.

Optional public/ assets:
- favicon-16x16.png
- favicon-32x32.png
- favicon-48x48.png
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png
- site.webmanifest

You can place those optional files in public/.
